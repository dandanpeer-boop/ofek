Life expectancy in the United States

The short answer: According to the CDC, life expectancy at birth in the United States was 79.0 years in 2024: 76.5 for males and 81.4 for females.

Life expectancy is a group average, not a forecast for one person. These figures come from the CDC's National Center for Health Statistics and describe the mortality of a single year.

The latest figures

The CDC's National Vital Statistics Reports (United States Life Tables, 2024) put life expectancy at birth at 79.0 years in 2024, up 0.6 year from 78.4 in 2023. For males it rose from 75.8 to 76.5, and for females from 81.1 to 81.4.

What a period life table means

A period life table asks what would happen to a group of people if they lived their whole lives under the death rates of one year, here 2024. It is a snapshot of current mortality, not a forecast of how long today's newborns will actually live.

Life expectancy at older ages

Life expectancy at birth includes people who die young. Someone who reaches 65 has already passed those risks: on 2024 mortality, a person aged 65 could expect to live 19.7 more years, for a total of 84.7. At 85 the average is 6.7 more years (91.7 in total), and at 100 it is 2.2 more years.

The gap between men and women

The difference in life expectancy between the sexes was 4.9 years in 2024. It narrowed from 7.8 years in 1979 to 4.8 in 2010, rose to 5.8 in 2021, and fell back to 4.9 in 2024.

Differences between groups

The same report gives 2024 life expectancy at birth of 81.8 for the Hispanic population, and, among non-Hispanic populations, 85.8 for Asian, 78.9 for White, 74.8 for Black and 71.1 for American Indian and Alaska Native people. These are group averages, and they say little about any one person.

What this does not tell you

A national average ignores your own habits, test results and family history. To see what the number looks like for you, the calculator starts from a life table for your age and sex and adjusts it to your habits and tests.

Myth or fact

"Life expectancy at birth is the age you will die"What do we know?

What we know: It is a group average. A person who reaches 65 can expect to live to about 84.7 on average, more than life expectancy at birth.

"Men and women live equally long"What do we know?

What we know: In 2024 females lived 4.9 years longer on average.
