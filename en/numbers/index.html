Your numbers

The short answer: Normal blood pressure is below 120/80, fasting glucose below 100 mg/dL and HbA1c below 5.7%. The only way to know is to measure.

Most of the factors that shorten life are silent at first. High blood pressure or high blood sugar don't hurt, so the only way to know is to measure. These are the numbers worth knowing, and the calculator can read them from a blood test file too.

Blood pressure

According to the ACC and AHA guideline (2017): normal is below 120/80, elevated is 120–129 systolic, and hypertension is 130/80 and above. Home measurements on several different days are more accurate than a single reading at the clinic.

Sugar

According to the American Diabetes Association: fasting glucose of 100–125 mg/dL or HbA1c of 5.7%–6.4% is prediabetes, and 126 or more or 6.5% or more is diabetes (confirmed by a repeat test).

Lipids and more

Target LDL cholesterol values depend on personal risk, so they are best set with a doctor. Lp(a) is usually tested once in a lifetime, because it is mainly determined by genetics.

What to do in practice: a routine blood test should include glucose, HbA1c, lipids and kidney function. You can measure blood pressure at home for three days, morning and evening, and write it down. Waist circumference is measured at the level of the navel, at the end of a normal breath out. You can enter the values in the calculator, or upload the test file: the values are read in the browser and the file is not sent anywhere.

Myth or fact

"If I feel fine, my blood pressure and sugar are normal"What do we know?

What we know: In most cases there are no symptoms in the early stages. Only measuring shows it.
