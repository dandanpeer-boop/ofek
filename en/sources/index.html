Sources and method

Last updated: 11/22/2026

How the calculator works, in brief

  • The starting point is the World Health Organization life table by country, age and sex.
  • Each risk factor is converted into a hazard ratio (HR) from a study, and multiplies the death rate at every age. The effect weakens at older ages.
  • Related factors are counted at reduced weight (1 / 0.85 / 0.7), and the combined ratio is limited to the range 0.4–8.
  • The result is shown as a range. It is a statistical estimate, not a personal forecast.

What the verification level means

  • Checked against the full text: The number in the calculator was compared with the full text of the paper.
  • Checked against the abstract: It was compared with the paper's abstract only, because the full text was not available to us.
  • Documented, not re-checked: Taken from earlier documentation of the build and not yet checked again. Marked openly.

Limitations and outside review

The model has not yet been reviewed by an actuary or a physician. The preliminary check on NHANES data is described on the "How it works" page, together with the limitations.
