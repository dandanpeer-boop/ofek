Life expectancy in Israel

The short answer: According to the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS), life expectancy in Israel in 2023 was 81.0 years for men and 85.5 for women. Excluding those killed in the Iron Swords war, it was 81.7 and 85.7.

Life expectancy is a group average, not a forecast for a particular person. To understand it you need to know which figure you are looking at: who measured it, for which year, and at which age.

The latest CBS figures

According to the CBS (Israel in Figures 2024), life expectancy at birth in 2023 was 81.0 years for men and 85.5 for women, compared with 80.7 and 84.8 in 2022. The CBS also notes that if those killed in the Iron Swords war are not counted, life expectancy was 81.7 for men and 85.7 for women.

Why the numbers differ between sources

The World Health Organization publishes a table for Israel for 2019, in which life expectancy at birth is 80.9 for men and 84.4 for women. The difference from the CBS stems mainly from a different year, and may also stem from the calculation method. The media quote different numbers also because of the difference between counting those killed in the war and not counting them. It is always worth checking the year, source and method.

Life expectancy by age

Life expectancy at birth is an average that also includes those who died young. Someone who has already reached a certain age has passed the early risks, so their expected age is higher. According to the WHO table for Israel (2019), at age 30 an average of about 51.6 years remain for men and about 54.8 for women. At age 50: 32.5 and 35.3. At age 65: 19.6 and 21.6. At age 80: 8.9 and 9.6.

At ages 60 and 85 these are values that the WHO publishes directly: 23.7 and 26.0 years remain at age 60, and 6.1 and 6.4 at age 85. The other ages are calculated from the same table.

The gap between men and women

The gap between the sexes in 2023 was about 4.5 years in favor of women. The gap shrinks with age: at age 80 it is about 0.7 years according to the table.

What these numbers do not say

A population's life expectancy does not take into account your habits, tests and family history. The CBS also publishes further breakdowns, for example by population group and region, and they are not included here because we have not examined them. To see what the number is for you, the calculator starts from the life table for your age and sex and adjusts it to habits and tests.

Myth or fact

"Life expectancy at birth is the age at which a person will die"What do we know?

What we know: It is a group average. Someone who reached 65 is expected to live on average to 84.6 if a man and 86.6 if a woman, more than life expectancy at birth.

"Men and women have the same life expectancy"What do we know?

What we know: In 2023 the gap was about 4.5 years in favor of women.
